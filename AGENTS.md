# Little Thread UI preferences

These preferences were explicitly established by the user:
- Use clear text actions instead of arrow icons, including reset arrows and decorative diagonal arrows.
- Do not use checkmark glyphs or visible native checkbox ticks. Use labelled switches for binary settings.
- Keep functional labels readable (normally 14 px or larger), with balanced alignment and consistent spacing.
- Keep the complete desktop sewing controls and hoop within a normal laptop viewport.
- Keep copy concise and purposeful. Do not add an eyebrow or promotional headline above the instructions.
- Use progress layout B by default: finish beside the stitch count, guide and start-over beneath.
- Keep the paper treatment for the progress area. Use physical stitched-linen samples and photographic spools in wooden compartments for the lower options, without paperclips. Keep icons and labels aligned.
- Use landscape reference photo placement B, with a dotted upload card behind it.
- Keep the Detail and Thread size titles on small paper labels above the wooden compartments.
